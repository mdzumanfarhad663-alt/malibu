Questions, answered

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is 3:00 PM. Check-out is 11:00 AM.

Is parking free?

Yes, parking is free.

Is WiFi included?

Yes, free WiFi is available.

Can I bring my pet?

Pets are welcome. Restrictions and fees apply. Contact us for details.

Is the pool heated?

Yes. The outdoor pool is heated and measures 20 × 40.

Is there a bar or restaurant onsite?

Yes. Crusty Pelican is attached to the property and serves pizza, wings, tropical drinks, and more.

What are the office hours?

Malibu is a limited-service property with reduced office hours. Contact us for current hours and arrival details.

How do I book direct?

Use any Book now button to check availability through Cloudbeds, or contact us directly.

How does Coastal Rewards work?

Join free for Bronze 10% off. Silver 15% follows 10 qualifying nights; Gold 20% follows 20 qualifying nights. Savings apply to eligible direct bookings. See the terms and conditions for details.
